Lab concept guide

What to observe while you experiment

Generalisation depends on the match between model capacity, data amount/noise and the underlying signal. Underfitting shows high error even on training data; overfitting shows a widening train–validation gap as the model captures idiosyncrasies.

MechanismChange model complexity, sample size or noise one at a time and compare training with validation behaviour.
Failure modeCalling any high training score “good fit” without checking unseen data, or diagnosing overfitting from a single noisy split.
VerificationTrack train and validation metrics across complexity and repeat/inspect variability; identify the region where added complexity stops improving validation.
Experiment deliberately
Increase complexity from too simple to very flexible. Predict the training curve and validation curve shape before running the sweep.
Bias–variance is visible. Increase polynomial degree or reduce data to see training error fall while validation error can rise.
