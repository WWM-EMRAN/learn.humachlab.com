Three ways to work

Learn, experiment, or solve a challenge.

Every modern playground can use the shared mode switch. Learn keeps the educational framing visible, Experiment encourages free manipulation, and Challenge adds an applied goal with a direct route to scored challenges.

LearnTrace controls, intermediate states, assumptions and explanations.
ExperimentManipulate freely and compare evidence across configurations.
ChallengeWork toward a concrete modelling goal and validate it without test-set peeking.
