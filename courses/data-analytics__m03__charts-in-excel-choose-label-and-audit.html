Charts in Excel Choose Label and Audit

An Excel chart maps worksheet values to visual encodings. Choosing a chart begins with the comparison: bars for category magnitudes, lines for ordered time trends, scatterplots for relationships between two quantitative variables, and distributions for shape/spread. Labels, axis scales and source ranges must then be audited so the graphic represents the intended data.

Learning goal: explain why Charts in Excel Choose Label and Audit behaves this way, apply it to a small example, and verify the result independently. Begin by being able to justify this first step: State the analytical comparison before choosing the chart type.
